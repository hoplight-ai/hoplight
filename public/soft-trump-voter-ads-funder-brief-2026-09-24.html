The strategy

His coalition rests on us versus them. The Epstein files and the Iran war are proof he is them.

The glue of the Trump coalition is one claim: he is on the side of regular people. This program does not argue policy and never sounds like the left. It shows soft 2024 Trump voters, inside their own values and in a voice they trust, that the swamp kept its secrets and the wars came anyway. The aim is dissonance, then an exit ramp with dignity: a regretful voter who stays home on November 3 rather than one who is asked to switch.

How the ads work. Static and short motion ads on Meta, from a neutral or right-of-center name, to about 800,000 soft Trump voters per state. Creative implies rather than asserts, which is what Meta's review has let through for ten months. Each week the losing variants are cut and the money moves to the winners. Nothing names a 2026 candidate, so the program stays inside a C4 and files nothing with the FEC.

3Messages and creative to test

The test

Five arms. Epstein in two frames, the Iran war in two frames, and a placebo. Randomized survey among 2024 Trump voters only, with an oversample of those who express regret. Rising costs can add two arms.

What it measures

Four outcomes. Enthusiasm to vote, Trump approval, regret, and favorability toward the Republican Senate candidate. The winning arm and frame go to ads; the rest stop there.

The two frames

Promise broken: he said one thing to you and did another. Elite protection: the club looks after its own and you pay. Each issue gets one of each.

Rules every piece follows. Imply, never assert, which is what ten months of Meta review has let through. No 2026 candidate named and no instruction about voting, which keeps the work inside a C4. Timeline to November 3

What changed since the July 2025 memo

The 2025 plan spent its first $85K on petition ads to build a list, then retargeted the list. The 2026 plan spends the first dollars on a randomized test and a measured turnout effect, because the election is forty days out and a list has no time to mature. The Iran war joins Epstein as a second proven betrayal. The petition and lead capture can ride along at no extra cost if the vehicle wants the list for 2028.

Choices for your advisers

  • Where. One Senate state, or a set of close House districts.
  • Measurement. Held-out areas with a short survey, or person-level randomization if the vehicle already texts.
  • Vehicle type. A C4 while ads name only Trump and the issues. Naming a 2026 candidate moves it to a PAC.
  • A third issue. Rising costs adds two test arms, about $6K to $12K.
  • Added channels. Volunteer sharing and image texts ride on the same creative.
