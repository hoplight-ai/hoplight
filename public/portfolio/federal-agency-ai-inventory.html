Key pattern: Biden-era actions (2022-Jan 2025) focused on risk mitigation, equity, transparency, and civil rights enforcement. Trump-era actions (Jan 2025+) shift toward deregulation, innovation promotion, and adoption acceleration. Most Biden-era binding regulations (HTI-1, AVM Rule, FCC ruling) remain in effect as they were finalized rules. Non-binding guidance and EO-dependent directives are most vulnerable to reversal. NIST technical work products (AI RMF, AI 100 series) persist as voluntary standards regardless of administration.
