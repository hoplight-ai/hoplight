This dashboard applies four lenses to the DNC's 2024 After Action Report: attention allocation (what did they spend words on), internal contradictions (what they admit vs. what they funded), structural omissions (what is missing entirely), and political economy (who benefits from the choices made).

Navigate with left/right arrow keys, the bottom buttons, or the top nav. Every finding is sourced to a specific page number in the report.

The Consultant Accountability Gap

30 vendors received $1.9 billion — 66.3% of all federal candidate spending. Average take: approximately $63 million per vendor.

Zero vendors are named critically. Zero have their performance evaluated.

"We have enough money to think and act differently." (p.140) The report reaches this conclusion without examining whether the current vendor structure enabled or prevented that possibility.
