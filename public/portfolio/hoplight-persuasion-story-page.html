So We Tested a Different Approach

Demographics vs. Psychographics

Psychographics let us see within demographics, where the persuasion opportunity actually lives.

Demographics

Who someone is.

Age, race, income, education.

What is this person's race?

vs

Psychographics

How someone makes sense of the world.

Values, beliefs, perceived threats, emotional triggers.

What does this person perceive as a threat?

Grow Progress Values Segments

Cut across race, class, and party: a Latino voter could be in any of these five groups.

Trusting Progressives

Believe in institutions, collective action, solidarity.

Anti-Authority Progressives

Left-leaning but deeply skeptical of power. Anti-corporate, anti-establishment.

Anxious Authoritarians

Seeking safety, order, protection from chaos.

Enterprising Conservatives

Value self-reliance, opportunity, meritocracy.

Religious Conservatives

Grounded in faith, tradition, moral structure.

Three Frames, Same Goal

We tested whether different psychological frames could activate different values segments.

Issue tested: "Many security officers are poorly paid and resourced."

Progressive

As first responders, our nation's nearly one million private security officers provide an essential service to our country, a non-police solution to community safety. To protect and care for the millions of people we encounter everyday, we should be fairly compensated and fully equipped.

That's why security officers from coast to coast are coming together across race to raise security industry standards. This means wages high enough to live on, more training and better equipment to make communities safer and the freedom to form unions, without management interference.

Personal Safety

Engine-generated ▼
Every day, you trust a security officer with something you care about. The office you leave after dark. The hospital where your parents get care. The school where your child walks through the doors. But the truth is, the people standing watch over us are too often underpaid, undertrained, and ignored by their companies. And that puts our safety at risk.

Meanwhile, big security companies rake in profits while cutting corners, leaving officers without the tools or backup they need.

Now officers are joining together, across race and region, demanding pay to stay sharp, training to stay safe, and the freedom to speak out. Because when they're respected and prepared, we're all safer. Protect the protectors, protect us all.

Pride / Valor

Engine-generated ▼
When danger strikes, when alarms sound, when something goes wrong, you don't call a CEO - you count on the security officer standing watch. Nearly a million men and women across America form a quiet line of defense, protecting our schools, hospitals, churches, stores, and workplaces.

But too often, those officers are sent into the fight without the pay, training, or equipment they need, while big security companies pocket profits, cut corners and silence those who speak up.

That's why officers are locking arms, shoulder to shoulder, demanding respect, fair pay, training, and the freedom to stand together. If we want strong communities, we need strong officers. Back the uniform. Back the officers. Back the people who keep America safe.

The Engine

We Already Know This Works

Deep canvassing proves that meeting people inside their worldview moves them. The problem is doing it at scale.

Relational Organizing / Deep Canvassing

A canvasser who listens, adapts, speaks in the listener's values: that works. But these tactics are prohibitively expensive to scale.

Proven but unscalable

Off-the-Shelf LLMs

Research shows large language models can persuade. But if the model changes, the message changes. No audit trail. No replicability.

Scalable but unaccountable

The Engine

Psychographic targeting with auditable infrastructure. The persuasion spec controls the output, the LLM writes to spec. Model-agnostic.

Accountable + Scalable

The Gap: Operationalization

We invest millions in research and message development. But implementing those learnings at scale is riddled with drift.

Four Auditable Layers

Built on 22 psychometric measurement traditions and 110 subscales. The four layers are the public map. The proprietary pipeline spans eight stages.

01

Trait Profile

Measures who the listener is psychologically. Takes signals in and outputs a factor vector.

02

Worldview

Projects the profile into felt worldview: archetype, antagonists, fears, and internal story.

03

Message Spec

Maps worldview directly to frame, narrative, metaphor, register, and appeal type.

04

Rendering

LLM writes prose to spec. The moat is the spec it receives from Layers 1-3.
