Every Political Dollar Buys Less

Federal election spending grew 5.1x from 2000 to 2024. General inflation grew 1.8x. The difference is not more democracy. It is higher unit costs, collapsing contact rates, and a media environment that consumes budgets faster than it delivers reach. The implication for political investors: strategy matters more than scale.

Corrections Applied to Reference Table

House avg. winner 2016: $1.0M → $1.5M (OpenSecrets). The original figure created a false 37% cost decline mid-trend.

Dark money 2016: $0.07B → $0.18B (OpenSecrets). Original conflated PAC contributions with total dark money spending.

Most expensive Senate, GA 2020: $470M → $510M+ (AdImpact/Governing). Understated by ~9%.

Most expensive Senate, OH 2024: $400M → $483M+ (AdImpact). Ad spending alone, excluding non-media expenditures.

Total ad spending 2024: ~$10.2B → ~$11.1B (AdImpact final). Original used pre-election projection, not final count.
