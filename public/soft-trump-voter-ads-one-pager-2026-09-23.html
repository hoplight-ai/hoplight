Soft Trump voters: a fast ad program for the last five weeks

Whit Pendergast, Hoplight. September 23, 2026, for donor and experiment advisers.

One in five 2024 Trump voters now regret their vote. The Iran war and rising costs top their reasons (Navigator Research, September 2026), and almost nobody is talking to them about it inside their own values or in a voice they would trust. Low-cost static and motion ads on Meta, from a neutral or right-of-center name, on the Epstein files and the Iran war, through November 3. A randomized message test first, then a held-out slice of the voter list so the effect on enthusiasm and turnout can be measured.

What it needs besides money

An existing C4 or PAC with a neutral or right-of-center brand, Meta's political ad approval already in hand, and an ad account with spending history. Its counsel clears each ad. Hoplight runs the campaign and does not own the vehicle.

Open choices for advisers

  • Where. One Senate state, or a set of close House districts.
  • Measurement. Held-out areas with a short survey, or person-level randomization if the vehicle already texts.
  • Vehicle type. A C4 while ads name only Trump and the issues. Naming a 2026 candidate moves it to a PAC.
  • A third issue. Rising costs adds two test arms, about $6K to $12K.
  • Added channels. Volunteer sharing and image texts ride on the same creative.
